# Parameters and why they have the values they have

Every value below is a choice (or inherited) unless marked as a constraint. Where a value was changed
during development, the measured reason is given. Defaults are the `call_centromeric_arrays.py` flags.

| parameter | value | flag | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chromosome set | NCBI assembly report rows `assembled-molecule` / `Chromosome` (MT excluded) | — | constraint: no length cut-off needed |
| minimum array | 10 kb | `--min-array` | satellome's `10kb` class. Shorter admits interstitial minisatellites; 100 kb loses fragmented FasTAN pieces (goat 816-bp arrays come as 100–300 kb pieces) |
| family identity / cover | 0.70 / 0.80 | `--ident`, `--cov` | cross-species level of our earlier rotation-aware family clustering; 0.80 identity splits alpha suprachromosomal families within one genome |
| family search | BLASTN word 7, e-value 1e-5, plus strand vs doubled + reverse-complement-doubled targets, 1 HSP per pair, 200 targets per query | `--aligner`, `--max-targets` | MMSeqs2 (the first choice) returned only self-hits for the largest active alpha arrays of CHM13 chr18, chr11, chr6 and chrX. 200 targets keeps goat (3,790 near-identical consensus) tractable; CHM13 loci identical to the unlimited search |
| short consensus | < 30 bp grouped by canonical rotation | `--short-len` | alignment is meaningless for micro/minisatellite units |
| superfamily | a sequence family joins the founder (largest family first by bp) whose base monomer period is 1× or 2× its own, ± 3 % × k; otherwise it founds a superfamily. No member-to-member linkage | `--period-tol` | bonobo (mat) alpha split into 18 sequence families and lost the breadth contest to a 32-bp subterminal satellite. k ≤ 2 because at k = 4 the window admits 703 ≈ 4 × 171. Founder, not single linkage: in bonobo pri, subterminal families of 62–141 bp chained in 3 % steps to ~86 bp, 2 × 86 ≈ 171, and a 70-bp satellite (41 arrays, 5.6 Mb, 8 chromosomes, no sequence hit to alpha) joined alpha; loci on chr14/17/22 sat 0.35–1.2 Mb from the chromosome start. With founders, chromosomes disjoint from the published active HOR: bonobo pat 6 → 0, bonobo pri 5 → 1; CHM13 unchanged |
| breadth: chromosome carries a superfamily | ≥ 10 kb of it (one array) | `--breadth-min-bp` | presence, not amount. At ≥ 100 kb bonobo mat alpha (present on 24 chromosomes; active HOR of chr1_mat 93 kb) counted 18, the 32-bp subterminal satellite also 18, and the satellite won on bp. Alpha at 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 kb: 24 / 23 / 21 / 18 chromosomes; the 32-bp satellite 19 / 19 / 18 / 18 |
| co-location, both ends, co-located families | ≥ 100 kb of a superfamily on the chromosome | `--chrom-min-bp` | alternatives not measured |
| centromeric superfamily | among superfamilies with a base period (a consensus ≥ `--short-len`), the one on the most chromosomes, ties by bp | — | breadth, not size: the largest array of a chromosome is HSat1/2/3, not alpha, on 8 of 24 CHM13 chromosomes. Short-consensus superfamilies are not eligible: under presence breadth the telomeric repeat (≥ 10 kb on all 28 sheep chromosomes) outranked SatI (26) and every sheep locus went to a chromosome end; the same repeat topped both giraffe haplotype assemblies. Such a superfamily is reported as `short_consensus_family_as_broad` in `exceptions.tsv` |
| co-family | breadth ≥ 0.75 × chosen; largest clusters within the merge gap on ≥ 0.80 of shared chromosomes | `--cofamily-breadth`, `--cofamily-coloc` | goat1.0 and goat2.0 (same individual) flipped between SatI and SatII by 30 vs 29 chromosomes; with co-families both carry SatI + SatII |
| period class | monomer period = k × family base period, k = 1..4, ± 3 % × k | `--period-tol` | CHM13 chr2/6/7/X: sequence hits just under 0.70 / 0.80 |
| locus merge gap | 1 Mb | `--merge-gap` | sensitivity, chromosomes whose locus is disjoint from the published set at 250 kb / 1 Mb / 2 Mb: CHM13 0 / 0 / 0 (live HOR inside our locus 21 / 21 / 22 of 24); chimpanzee pri 0 / 0 / 0; bonobo pat 1 / 0 / 0; siamang pri 6 / 3 / 3; sheep (authors' gene span) 9 / 9 / 8. Rerun with `CALLER_FLAGS="--merge-gap 250000" bash workflow/call_all.sh …` |
| cap mode | chosen superfamily in both terminal 10 % (≥ 100 kb each) on > 0.5 of its chromosomes → interior cluster (midpoint 10–90 %) preferred | — | siamang alpha forms multi-Mb subterminal caps on 23/26 chromosomes; without cap mode every siamang locus was a cap. Off for acrocentric genomes |
| ambiguous locus | second cluster ≥ 50 % of the first | `--ambiguous-frac` | flag only |
| terminal locus | within 1 % of an end | `--end-frac` | flag only (telocentric / acrocentric) |
